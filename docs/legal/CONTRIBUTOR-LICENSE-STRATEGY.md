# Contributor Licensing Strategy Proposal

> Status: proposal only. Legal and maintainer review is required before adoption. This document is
> not a contract and does not create a Contributor License Agreement.

Kosmos is distributed under the PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0 while preserving the
possibility of separately authorized commercial licensing. External contributions can complicate
that option unless the project receives sufficient rights from every contributor.

Before actively soliciting external contributions, the maintainer should obtain qualified legal
review and choose one of these documented approaches:

1. Adopt a narrowly scoped individual and corporate CLA granting the project the rights needed to
   redistribute contributions under the project license and possible future commercial terms while
   contributors retain copyright.
2. Require copyright assignment under a separately reviewed agreement where appropriate.
3. Accept contributions only under the existing project license and accept that future relicensing
   may require permission from every copyright holder.

The first approach is the current engineering recommendation because it can preserve contributor
ownership while providing relicensing flexibility, but its terms, signatory entity, privacy
handling, records, and dispute law require legal decisions. No CLA bot or click-through acceptance
should be enabled without explicit authorization and an approved final agreement.

Every pull request should still require a contributor to confirm authorship, authority to submit,
and absence of undisclosed incompatible material. That confirmation is not a substitute for an
approved CLA.
