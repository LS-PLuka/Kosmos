# Trademark and Visual Identity Policy Proposal

> Status: proposal only. Maintainer and legal approval are required. This document does not assert
> that any Kosmos mark is registered and does not grant trademark rights.

Software source licensing, copyright in visual assets, and rights in names or marks are separate.
The PolyForm Noncommercial License applies to the software as stated in the repository; it should
not be interpreted as permission to imply endorsement or to use Kosmos names and identity as the
branding of a modified distribution.

A future approved policy should define:

- nominative use when accurately referring to the Kosmos project;
- attribution requirements for unmodified redistribution;
- naming and visual differentiation required for forks and modified distributions;
- prohibited uses that imply sponsorship, affiliation, or endorsement;
- rules for logos, the institutional symbol, Kosmos Core, animation, and merchandise;
- a clear request channel for uses outside the policy; and
- treatment of community events, screenshots, reviews, and compatibility statements.

Before publication, confirm ownership and provenance, conduct an availability review, decide who
can grant permissions, and have the final policy reviewed. Do not claim registration or ownership
of third-party resources without evidence.
